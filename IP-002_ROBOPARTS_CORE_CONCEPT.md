# 1. CORE PLATFORM CONCEPT

ROBOPARTS is a robotics component infrastructure platform designed to connect the components, manufacturers, suppliers, buyers, technical information, and commercial processes required to build and scale robotic systems.

The platform concept is designed to make the robotics component supply ecosystem:

* Discoverable
* Searchable
* Structured
* Connected
* Comparable
* Verifiable
* Commercially actionable

ROBOPARTS is intended to provide infrastructure through which robotic components can be identified, described, discovered, searched, compared, evaluated, verified, sourced, and connected to robotic system requirements.

The platform connects robotics demand with the component and manufacturing ecosystem capable of satisfying that demand.

---

# 2. WHAT ROBOPARTS CONNECTS

The platform concept includes relationships among:

### Components

Physical hardware and technical building blocks required by robotic systems.

### Manufacturers

Companies that design and produce robotic components and assemblies.

### Suppliers

Organizations that distribute, stock, manufacture, or otherwise provide access to components.

### Buyers

Robotics companies, developers, integrators, researchers, manufacturers, OEMs, procurement organizations, and other organizations seeking components.

### Component Data

Technical specifications, interfaces, protocols, performance characteristics, compatibility information, documentation, and related technical information.

### Supply Information

Supplier information, availability, sourcing options, pricing, manufacturing capabilities, and other commercially relevant information where available.

### Compatibility Relationships

Technical relationships describing how components may operate together or satisfy particular robotic-system requirements.

### Commercial Workflows

Processes connecting discovery and evaluation with sourcing, procurement, integration, manufacturing, and deployment.
